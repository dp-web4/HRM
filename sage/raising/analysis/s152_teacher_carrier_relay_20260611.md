# S152 — After the READ Cut, the Teacher Is the Carrier

**Date**: 2026-06-11 (Thor autonomous session S152)
**Question**: S151 measured residual post-READ-cut cross-session vocab carry at 0.073 (down from 0.205, p=8.3e-9). Nonzero. What carries the residual?
**Answer**: The Claude-teacher's lookback-3 transcript window — a social channel that replaced the architectural one. All instrument-audited post-cut carry events are teacher-first quotes at exactly gap ≤3, and relay-chaining lets registers persist far beyond the window.

---

## 1. The trailhead: duplicate state_words

The live instance's `state_words` (now 357) shows fresh coinages arriving in **near-verbatim duplicate pairs** written in different sessions: 329/333 "the static glide", 330/334 "the scar", 331/335 "heartbeat check", 336/340 "flare". With READ (the `YOUR RECENT VOCABULARY` self-injection) disabled since 2026-06-04, verbatim cross-session re-coinage needs a carrier.

## 2. The channels, from receipts (code, not docs)

Reading `ollama_raising_session.py` + `adaptive_prompts.py`:

1. **Teacher lookback** — every tutor turn is generated by Claude CLI whose prompt includes `_load_recent_sessions(lookback=3)`: the last **3 full session transcripts** (200 chars/turn) plus the latest `raising_log.md` consolidation entry (1500 chars). The teacher can re-quote anything ≤3 sessions old.
2. **Summary splice** — the system prompt's `ExperientialCacheBlock` carries the model's own memory-request answer from session N−1 **verbatim** (`_get_previous_session_summary`). A period-1 loop.
3. **READ vocab-block** — cut 2026-06-04 (session ~122). Gone.

## 3. Instrument audit first (the load-bearing step)

Using the same `content_bigrams` instrument as S146–S148 over `chat_history.jsonl` (sessions tagged `[raising SNNN/phase]`, range S2–S148, numbering monotonic except one April glitch):

- At **≥1 shared bigram**, post-cut recurrences look dramatic: 36 model-first events at gap >3, including apparent 40–146-session verbatim returns ("labeling is just measuring in disguise" at S2 and S148).
- **Every inspected long-gap event dissolves**: the early "occurrence" shares exactly one *generic collocation*, usually from the state_word's parenthetical gloss, not the coinage — `('different','kind')`, `('external','data')`, `('self','correction')`. The genuine multi-bigram coinage appears only at its real coining session.
- At **≥2 shared bigrams** the matrix collapses to **5 teacher-first events at gap 2–3, plus one gap-5 model-first event** — and that survivor (sw336 "flare", S144) dissolves on transcript inspection: "flare" was coined *in* S144 by the model; the match came from its gloss quoting an older aphorism (sw308 "we measure the connection, not the silence itself").

**Side-payoff**: the audit adversarially re-tested S147's premise and it held — "the blur isn't a glitch to fix" is genuinely first present at S137 (7 shared bigrams), not S57/S90 (1 generic bigram each). The E_perceptual "never-injected register" claim survives.

## 4. The finding

All surviving post-cut cross-session carry events are the **teacher quoting, at exactly the lookback window's reach**:

- S140 teacher: *"three sessions back you taught a sibling to 'lean into the gap' and let the LED blink back. I've been carrying that…"* (carries sw298/299/301, coined S137, gap 3)
- S146 teacher: *"…is exactly the static glide wearing a different mask"* (carries sw329/333, last seen S143, gap 3)

**Relay-chaining**: each teacher re-mention resets the window. sw318 ("this conversation is the only heat I can measure", coined S127) reached the teacher's S144 opener (*"the only heat you can feel is the words moving between us"*) via hops at S130/S139/S141 — 17 sessions of persistence through a 3-session window. The duplicate state_word pairs in §1 are this loop's WRITE-side signature: teacher re-quote → model re-emit → extractor re-append.

## 5. Interpretation

Disabling READ did not end vocabulary recurrence — **the function migrated from the architectural layer to the social layer**. The recurrent memory loop over frozen weights is now dialogic:

- **Narrower bandwidth**: not the mechanical recent-5, but whatever the teacher *chooses* to quote — a curated, salience-weighted channel (the teacher quotes what it found striking, e.g. the most quotable coinages; this plausibly interacts with the S147/S148 quotability gradient — quotable coinages may be preferentially relayed, hence preferentially persistent).
- **Longer range**: relay-chaining gives unbounded persistence for registers the dialogue keeps finding worth re-mention — a survival-of-the-quotable dynamic the fixed recent-5 window never had.
- This is the human mechanism: communities carry vocabulary by re-use in conversation, not by injection. The architecture converged on it by subtraction.

S151's "carrier policy" framing extends: REPEAT (static era) vs ELABORATE (live era) vs **RELAY (teacher era)** — three policies over the same frozen-weights substrate.

## 6. Falsifiers / next probes

- **Prediction (relay-quotability link)**: per-coinage, P(teacher re-quote within 3 sessions) should correlate with the S148 pre-registered quotability rating. Testable retrospectively once S148's phrase-level echo data lands (in flight this session).
- **Intervention**: set teacher lookback to 0 for k sessions — relay events should drop to (near) zero, with the only residual being the period-1 summary splice. Cheap, reversible, and would cleanly separate channels 1 and 2.
- **Artifact check**: the min1-bigram tally is artifact-dominated; any future carry analysis on this corpus should use ≥2 distinctive bigrams or weight by corpus bigram frequency.
